My research group brings together scholars and emerging academics who share a commitment to rigorous inquiry into international relations, foreign policy, regional integration, and security and development in Africa. I welcome collaboration with researchers, postgraduate candidates, and policy practitioners who are working on questions at the intersection of politics, regional cooperation, and public life.

If you are interested in working with me, feel free to reach out via email at [ukouwak@uniuyo.edu.ng](mailto:ukouwak@uniuyo.edu.ng).
