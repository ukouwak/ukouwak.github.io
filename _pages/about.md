Dr. Uko Uwak is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Political Science and Public Administration, Faculty of Social Sciences, University of Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. He joined the University in 2012. He holds a B.Sc. (Hons) from the University of Uyo (1998), an M.Sc. in International Relations from Uyo (2002) and a Ph.D. in International Relations from the University of Calabar (2008).

His research covers regional integration in West Africa (ECOWAS), foreign policy, regional security and terrorism, migration and refugees, and food security.

## Research Interests
- International Relations
- Regional Integration and ECOWAS
- Foreign Policy Analysis
- Security and Conflict Studies
- Migration and Refugee Studies

## Professional Memberships
- Member, Nigerian Political Science Association
- Fellow, Institute of Policy Management Development
